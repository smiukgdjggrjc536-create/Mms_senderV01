1. @/services/* alias → maps to OLD services/ dir, NOT src/services/.
   AI modules (engine.js, restockWorker.js, circuitBreaker.js) are in
   src/services/. Use RELATIVE imports from src/app/ or src/lib/:
   e.g. import { getStats } from '../../services/ai/engine.js'
   The @/lib/* alias works fine for src/lib/ imports.
